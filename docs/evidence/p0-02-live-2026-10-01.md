### 2. write privileges held by anon / authenticated / service_role

```sql
SELECT grantee, table_name, privilege_type
FROM information_schema.role_table_grants
WHERE table_schema='public'
  AND grantee IN ('anon','authenticated','service_role')
  AND privilege_type IN ('INSERT','UPDATE','DELETE','TRUNCATE')
ORDER BY grantee, table_name, privilege_type;
```

Full raw dump at the bottom of this file. Key facts, all checkable there:

- **`TRUNCATE` appears for NO role on ANY table** — migration 013 (PR #26)
  `REVOKE TRUNCATE` is live; behavioral proof in test 5.
- The 010-hardened tables (`financial_annual`, `ingestion_log`,
  `rishi_snapshots`, `signal_history`) and the 012 security-master tables
  (`securities`, `symbol_history`, `universe`) appear **only** under
  `service_role` — anon and authenticated hold no write privileges on them.
- Honest note (a gap, not a pass): the 001–009-era tables still carry
  blanket grants for anon/authenticated; denial rests on their RLS
  policies (all enabled per test 1). Re-auditing those policies row by row
  is L5-05 work and is recorded as a standing item, NOT claimed as
  verified here.
